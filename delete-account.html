Delete your Curatio account

This page explains how to request deletion of your Curatio account and everything stored with it. You do not need the app installed to make this request.

How to request deletion

  1. Email hsiaorendy@gmail.com from the email address you used to sign up for Curatio.
  2. Use the subject line Delete my account.
  3. Send it. You don't need to include anything else — the address you send from is what identifies your account.
Email the deletion request

Requests are processed within 30 days, and you'll get a confirmation email once it's done. If you send from a different address than the one on the account, we'll ask you to verify ownership before deleting anything.

What gets deleted

Everything tied to your account is permanently removed:

DataWhat it is
Account informationYour email address and authentication record
Focus sessionsStart/end times, durations, subject, and completion outcome
Subjects & goalsYour subjects, their colors, and your daily focus-time goals
Allow-listThe names and contact identifiers of people you allowed through
FlashcardsQuestions, answers, and spaced-repetition review history
To-do itemsDaily plan items you created
Leaderboard entriesYour daily focus totals, including the anonymous label they appeared under

What is kept, and for how long

  • Nothing is retained after deletion is complete beyond what the law requires. Your rows are removed from our database; there is no archived copy kept for analytics or marketing.
  • Encrypted backups of the database may still contain your data for up to 30 days after deletion, after which they roll off automatically. These backups are not queryable and are used only for disaster recovery.
  • Purchase records. If you subscribed to Curatio Plus, Google Play — not Curatio — holds the transaction record, and tax law requires it be retained. Deleting your Curatio account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel that separately in the Play Store, under Payments & subscriptions.

Deleting some data without deleting your account

You can clear individual items — a subject, a flashcard, a to-do, or someone on your allow-list — from inside the app at any time, without deleting your account. Deleting your account is all-or-nothing and cannot be undone.

What Curatio never collects

For completeness, some things simply don't exist to delete. Curatio does not read the content of your notifications, does not request bulk access to your contacts, does not collect location data, and contains no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs.

Full detail is in the Curatio Privacy Policy.
